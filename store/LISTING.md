## Full description (4000 characters max)

```
Bulk Clock is a nutrition and training tracker that works entirely on your phone.

No account. No subscription. No ads. Nothing you log is uploaded anywhere — which
also means nobody else has a copy, so take a backup now and then.

WHAT IT DOES

• Calorie and macro targets from your own biometrics, using the Mifflin–St Jeor
  equation — then, once you have a few weeks of data, measured properly from your
  real intake and weight trend instead of a formula.
• A food database of 280+ items, including 89 Moroccan dishes that no
  international tracker knows: tagine, couscous, harira, msemen, rfissa, zaalouk.
• Barcode scanning, custom foods, and your own recipes with proper serving sizes,
  so a family tagine logs as a quarter of itself rather than all of it.
• Any number of meals a day — two or eight, named and timed however your day
  actually runs. Nothing in the app assumes three.
• A training programme you build yourself: your own days, your own exercises,
  your own sets and reps. Four templates to start from if you want one.
• Set-by-set logging with RPE, warm-up and drop sets, estimated 1RM, automatic
  personal records, and a progression suggestion that tells you when to add
  weight and why.
• Reminders that arrive thirty minutes before each meal and session, answerable
  from the notification itself, plus a full-screen alert for the one that matters.
• A daily check-in that asks what actually happened — and a consistency figure
  built from your answers rather than from wishful thinking.
• Water, supplements, body measurements, progress photos, a weekly review and a
  month calendar of everything you kept.
• Full dark and light themes, metric or imperial.

WHAT IT DOESN'T DO

No streak-loss guilt. No social feed. No "you've broken your chain" messages. A
missed day lowers a percentage slightly; it doesn't destroy anything. This is a
tool for people trying to gain weight deliberately, and the framing matters.

FOR MOROCCO, PROPERLY

Most trackers make you guess at a plate of couscous. Bulk Clock has real figures
for the food people here actually eat, from typical home-cooked recipes.

YOUR DATA

Everything lives in the app's private storage on your device. Export it to JSON
or CSV whenever you want. Uninstalling deletes all of it. The only time the app
uses the internet is when you scan a barcode or let it fetch a food photo.

Bulk Clock provides general nutrition and training information. It is not medical
advice. If you have a medical condition or a history of disordered eating, speak
to a professional first.
```
